MASTER'S THESIS · 2026

Collective Dynamics of Colliding Inertial Particles in Prescribed Carrier Flows

Six-month research internship with Team CALISTO at Inria Sophia Antipolis, supervised by Dr Christophe Henry and Dr Laetitia Giraldi.

I developed Lagrangian solvers for passive tracers and finite-size inertial particles, coupling continuous Stokes-drag dynamics with discrete hard-particle collisions through operator splitting.

About Team CALISTO ↗
THE QUESTION

How do carrier-flow structure, inertia and collisions change clustering, dispersion and particle redistribution?

THE APPROACH

Prescribed cellular and shear flows; reconstructed near-wall turbulent-channel DNS; Eulerian density fields and Lagrangian particle and pair statistics.

THE DIAGNOSTICS

Density correlations, cluster morphology, collision-angle selection, same-pair recurrence and bootstrap uncertainty estimates.

WORK IN PROGRESS

First-author manuscript in preparation with Laetitia Giraldi and Christophe Henry.
