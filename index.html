MASTER’S THESIS · 2026

Collective Dynamics of Colliding Inertial Particles in Prescribed Carrier Flows

My thesis combines verified hard-particle solvers, controlled-flow simulations and a reduced near-wall turbulence model. I study how inertia and collisions modify clustering, pair organisation and spatial coherence.

Matched simulations with collisions enabled and disabled provide a common reference for distinguishing collision effects from structures imposed by the carrier flow.

SELECTED COMPUTATIONAL PROJECTS

Numerical methods in practice

01

UNIVERSITY OF HAMBURG · 2025

Adaptive micro–macro traffic simulation

A hybrid framework coupling the Cell Transmission Model and Intelligent Driver Model through adaptive resolution switching. I implemented a Godunov finite-volume solver and mass-preserving interfaces, with validation against exact solutions and conservation checks.

Conservation laws · Finite-volume methods · Multi-fidelity modelling

02

UNIVERSITÉ CÔTE D’AZUR · 2026

Hyperparameter optimisation with approximate gradients

Implemented HOAG for regularised logistic regression and RBF kernel ridge regression using implicit differentiation, L-BFGS-B and matrix-free conjugate gradients. I compared optimisation methods under a fixed computational budget.

Bilevel optimisation · Implicit differentiation · Python
